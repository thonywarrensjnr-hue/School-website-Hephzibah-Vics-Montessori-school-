Admission Process

Simple 4-step process to join our Montessori family

Step 1: Inquiry & Application

Submit the online application form or visit our admissions office. You'll need to provide basic information about your child and family.

  • Complete online application form
  • Pay application fee of ₦5,000
  • Submit required documents including payment receipt

Step 2: School Interaction

Your child will participate in an age-appropriate interaction session with our Montessori directresses.

  • Observation of child in prepared environment
  • Parent interview with principal
  • Assessment of developmental readiness

Step 3: Admission Decision

Our admission committee reviews all applications and makes decisions based on multiple factors.

  • Committee review within 2 weeks
  • Communication of decision via email
  • If accepted, admission offer letter sent

Step 4: Enrollment & Orientation

Complete the enrollment process and participate in orientation programs.

  • Submit enrollment documents
  • Pay tuition deposit
  • Attend parent orientation
  • Child's gradual entry program

Required Documents

Checklist of documents needed for admission

For All Applicants

  • Completed application form
  • Copy of child's birth certificate
  • Copy of child's immunization records
  • Passport-sized photographs (4 copies)
  • Previous school reports (if applicable)
  • Parents' identification documents
  • Payment Receipt (Mandatory) - Proof of ₦5,000 application fee payment

Medical Requirements

  • Medical examination form (school provided)
  • Immunization certificate
  • Allergy information (if any)
  • Special needs documentation (if applicable)
  • Emergency contact information
  • Health insurance details
