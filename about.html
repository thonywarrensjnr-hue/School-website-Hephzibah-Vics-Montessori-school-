About Our School

Discover our history, mission, and vision for quality education

Our Story

Founded in 2016, Hephzibah Vics Montessori began with a simple vision: to create a nurturing environment where children could develop their full potential. Starting with just 20 students, we've grown into a premier educational institution serving over 1500 students.

Our name "Hephzibah" means "my delight is in her," reflecting our commitment to making education a delightful experience for every child.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic Montessori education that develops independent, confident, and compassionate individuals who are prepared to excel in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To be the leading Montessori institution recognized for academic excellence, character development, and innovation in early childhood education.

Our Values

Respect, Integrity, Excellence, Compassion, and Innovation form the foundation of everything we do at Hephzibah Vics Montessori.

Montessori Philosophy

Understanding the Montessori approach to education

The Montessori Method

Developed by Dr. Maria Montessori, our educational approach is child-centered and based on scientific observations of children from birth to adulthood. Key principles include:

  • Respect for the Child: Children are treated as individuals
  • Absorbent Mind: Children learn effortlessly from their environment
  • Sensitive Periods: Windows of opportunity for learning
  • Prepared Environment: Classrooms designed for optimal learning
  • Autoeducation: Children can teach themselves
  • Freedom within Limits: Structured choice in learning
  • Individualized Learning: Pace and style suited to each child
  • Practical Life Skills: Real-world activities for development
