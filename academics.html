Curriculum Details

Comprehensive learning experiences across all age groups

Toddler Community (1.5 - 3 Years)

The Toddler program focuses on developing independence, language skills, and social awareness. Our prepared environment allows toddlers to explore safely while developing coordination and concentration.

Daily Schedule:

7:00 - 8:00 AM Arrival & Free Play
8:30 - 9:30 AM Practical Life Activities
9:30 - 10:00 AM Circle Time
10:00 - 10:30 AM Snack Time
10:30 - 11:30 AM Outdoor Play
11:30 AM - 1:00 PM Story Time & Departure

Learning Objectives:

  • Develop independence and self-care skills
  • Build vocabulary and communication skills
  • Enhance fine and gross motor skills
  • Learn social skills and cooperation
  • Develop concentration and order

Nursery Program (3 - 6 Years)

The Nursery program offers a complete Montessori curriculum with five key areas of learning. Children work individually and in small groups, developing at their own pace.

Curriculum Areas:

  • Practical Life: Care of self and environment
  • Sensorial: Refinement of the senses
  • Language: Reading, writing, and communication
  • Mathematics: Concrete to abstract concepts
  • Cultural Studies: History, Geography, science, art, music

Assessment:

  • Continuous observation-based assessment
  • Individual progress tracking
  • Bi-Weekly Test
  • Portfolio of student work
  • Development milestone tracking

Primary/Secondary Program (6 - 14 Years)

The Primary/Secondary program follows the Montessori cosmic education approach, helping children understand the interconnectedness of all knowledge. Students engage in deep research, collaborative projects, and advanced academic work.

Core Subjects:

  • Mathematics: Algebra, Geometry, Problem-solving
  • English Studies: Literature, Grammar, Composition
  • Basic Science: Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • Basic Technology: Design, Workshop skills
  • ICT: Computer science, Digital literacy
  • Agricultural Science: Farming, Crop production
  • Social Studies: History, Geography, Civic Education
  • CRS: Moral and religious education
  • PHE: Physical and health education
  • Business Studies: Entrepreneurship, Commerce
  • Home Economics: Food and nutrition, Sewing
  • French: Foreign language learning
  • Music & Fine Arts: Creative expression
  • Coding & Robotics: Programming, AI, Machine Learning

Assessment Methods:

  • Continuous assessment throughout term
  • Project-based evaluations
  • Mid-term tests and examinations
  • Research presentations
  • Portfolio reviews
  • Standardized assessments

Term Structure:

  • Term 1: September - December
  • Term 2: January - April
  • Term 3: May - August

Learning Approach:

Our Primary/Secondary program combines Montessori principles with the Nigerian national curriculum. Students learn through:

  • Hands-on experiments and investigations
  • Collaborative group projects
  • Individual research and presentations
  • Field trips and community engagement
  • Technology-integrated learning
  • Leadership and mentorship opportunities
