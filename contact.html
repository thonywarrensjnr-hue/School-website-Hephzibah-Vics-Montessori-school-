Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your school hours? +

Regular school hours are from 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Extended care is available from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM for working parents.

Do you offer transportation services? +

Yes, we offer safe and reliable transportation services covering most areas in the city. Our buses are equipped with GPS tracking and safety belts.

What is the teacher-student ratio? +

We maintain a low teacher-student ratio of 1:8 for Toddler Program, 1:10 for Primary Program, and 1:12 for Elementary Program to ensure personalized attention.

Do you have after-school programs? +

Yes, we offer a variety of after-school programs including sports, music, dance, art, STEM activities, and homework assistance.
